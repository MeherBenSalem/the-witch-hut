# Witchbound 2.0.0 "Every Gift Has a Price"

*The Witch Hut* is now **Witchbound** (listed as **Remnants | Witchbound**), a witchcraft RPG. The mod id is unchanged
(`better_witch_huts`) and 1.0 worlds keep loading.

## New

- **Seven coven huts**, each placed only where the land suits it:
  - The Old Hut (the 1.0 hut, repaired)
  - Bog Hut
  - Mangrove Roost
  - Hedge Cottage
  - Nightshade Circle
  - Rime Hut
  - Bone Hut
- **Resident Coven Witches**, one per hut, with names. They never despawn, never join raids and fight only if provoked.
  Right-click one to open the **Coven screen**.
- **Pacts:** 12 permanent bargains, each a Gift and a Price. Three are offered per resident per day, with 1 to 3 slots
  depending on your standing. They survive death and milk, and a resident undoes one for 24 emeralds.
- **Hexes:** 8 lasting curses.
  - Witches channel them in battle; hitting the witch breaks the spell.
  - They survive death and milk.
  - Each rolls a task that breaks it. Witch Bottles and residents can break them too.
- **Coven Standing** from −100 to 100, in six tiers. Kin are left alone by wild witches.
- **Errands** (20 kinds), **offerings** and **remedies** at every resident.
- **Items:** Grimoire (your ledger), Witch Bottle (breaks a hex), Poppet (catches a hex).
- Pacts and hexes appear as **status-effect sigils** on the HUD.
- **Advancements:** a 25-advancement "Witchbound" tab.
- **Config:** `config/better_witch_huts.json` with comments.
- **Commands:** `/witchbound` and operator tools.
- **Minecraft 26.3** (Fabric and NeoForge).

## Changed

- The 1.0 hut no longer has a witch spawner: a brewing stand and Granny Hollowell live there now. Its chest and barrels
  draw from loot tables (1.0 had fixed iron in one chest).
- The hut generates in plains, sunflower plains, forests and birch forests. Swamps and dark forests have their own huts
  now.
- Huts are terrain-aware and far less rare. 1.0 used spacing 150; the new huts use 14 to 30 chunks per hut type.
- **No dependencies.** 1.0 required `jauml` without saying so.

## Compatibility

- Same mod id and same `better_witch_huts:witch_hut` structure id. Half-generated 1.0 huts finish from the unchanged
  1.0 template.
- Works alongside YUNG's Better Witch Huts and other swamp-hut mods.
- Fabric 26.1.2 / 26.2 / 26.3 (needs Fabric API) and NeoForge 26.1.2 / 26.2 / 26.3. Java 25.
